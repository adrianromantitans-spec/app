Delete your account

Sign in to the account you want to close, then use the button below and type its email to confirm. The action cannot be undone. You can also open Account → Delete account in the app.

Delete my account

What happens after successful deletion

  • The old account is disabled, its sign-in profile is cleared and its sessions are revoked. This does not mean that you are permanently prohibited from registering a new account.
  • Your profile name, notifications and push subscriptions are removed. Shared groups retain a technical account identifier under the label “Cuenta eliminada” so balances and records remain coherent.
  • Shared expenses, payment records, history and receipts remain available to the other members. Deleting the account does not cancel debts or remove every mention of you in descriptions and attachments.
  • Groups where you are the only member are removed from the app. Associated stored files also require completion of deletion through the storage provider.
  • If you administer a shared group, another member becomes administrator.
  • Remaining personal information, receipt files and provider records can be reviewed through support. Profile de-identification is not a guarantee of complete anonymization; backups follow provider deletion cycles.

If deletion fails, is unavailable, or you cannot access the account, contact support@divzplit.com to request help or a wider erasure review. Do not send passwords. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data does not delete the account.
