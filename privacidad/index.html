Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains which data DivZplit uses to provide accounts, shared groups and expense management, and how to contact us to exercise your rights.

1. Data we use

  • Account: email address, display name, user identifier, language, self-declared age band, terms version and acknowledgement details, and technical authentication data. If you choose Google sign-in, DivZplit receives the basic identity data you authorize, such as your identifier, name and email address. DivZplit does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Shared content: group names and members, invitations, expenses, amounts, currencies, participants, budgets, repayments, payment requests, tasks, polls, shopping lists and activity history.
  • Receipts: images you choose to attach and information you add to expenses. Automatic receipt recognition runs in your browser; an attached image may be saved in the group's storage.
  • Notifications: preferences, language, push subscription and technical data needed to deliver notifications when you enable them.
  • Technical use and support: browser storage for your session and app functionality. Providers may process IP addresses and technical security logs. If you contact support, your email address and message content will be processed.

2. Purposes and legal bases

Data needed to create your account, save group content and provide the features you request is processed to perform the service. Security, abuse prevention and incident handling rely, where appropriate, on the legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users. Push notifications are optional and enabled with your permission; you can disable them in the app or browser. Where the law requires information to be retained or disclosed, processing relies on that legal obligation.

3. Who can access your data

Group members can access the content shared within their group. Administrative functions allow certain changes and deletions. Only share data you have the right to include; receipts may contain information about other people. The controller may process data needed to handle incidents, manage rights requests and maintain the service.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, database, receipt storage and server functions. The primary database is hosted in Ireland (eu-west-1); this does not mean every provider operation remains in the EU.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • Resend: authentication emails sent through Supabase.
  • Google: optional sign-in. Support email uses Google's email service.
  • Cloudflare: domain management and support email forwarding.
  • Browser or device push services: notification delivery if you enable it.

Exchange-rate requests use reference sources from the ECB and ExchangeRate-API without needing the group's personal expenses or balances. Providers may operate in different countries. Processing and international transfers must have the safeguards required by applicable law and are subject to each provider's contracts and terms.

5. Retention and deletion

Account data and shared content are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and address applicable requests or responsibilities. Deleting a notification or clearing a visible list does not delete confirmed payments or all group records. This version does not erase accounts automatically. Use Contact in your account to request erasure by email, or contact support directly. A request is received only when your email reaches support. Requests will be reviewed with regard to shared data, other members' rights and legal obligations. Providers may retain technical copies during their backup and deletion cycles.

6. Your rights and choices

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw consent for processing that relies on it. Contact support@divzplit.com; we may ask for the minimum information needed to verify your identity. You can export groups available in your profile in JSON format and manage notification permissions. Where enabled, an expanded account export is also available; receipt image files, provider logs and other data outside that export can be requested from support. Requests are normally answered within one calendar month, subject to applicable legal exceptions and verification where reasonably necessary. You may lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority in the country where you live or work, or where the alleged infringement took place. In Romania, you can contact the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP).

7. Browser storage and security

The app uses local and session storage to maintain access, support secure authentication and keep operational or demo data. The current code does not include third-party advertising or analytics tools. Clearing browser data removes local information or signs you out, but does not by itself delete your account data on the server. Access controls, HTTPS connections and group permissions are used; no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Updates and contact

This policy will be updated when features or data processing change. The update date appears at the top. Questions and requests can be sent to support@divzplit.com.

9. Children and advertising

Registration is available only to people aged 16 or older. Users who declare they are under 16 cannot continue through the registration form. We collect an age band, not a date of birth. This is a self-declaration, not verified age assurance. If you believe an account belongs to someone under 16, contact support so that we can review it. Advertising is planned but no advertising or tracking SDK has been activated. Optional advertising tracking will require a separate provider-specific review and any required permission; accepting the terms or enabling notifications does not authorize advertising.

10. Data controller and contact

The data controller is Adrian Roman, based in Romania. Privacy contact: support@divzplit.com. Website: divzplit.com.
