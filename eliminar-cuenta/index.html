Delete your account

You can delete your DivZplit account yourself, in the app or on this website. You do not need to email support.

Delete my account

What happens

  • You are signed out on every device and cannot sign in again with this email, Google or Apple identity.
  • Your name, notifications, push subscriptions and profile data are removed.
  • In shared groups your name becomes “Cuenta eliminada”. Other people’s expenses and balances stay.
  • A group that only you belong to is deleted, including its receipt photos.
  • If you administer a group with other people, another member becomes the administrator.
  • Receipt photos in groups you share are kept for the other members.

Sign in with the account you want to delete, then type that email to confirm. This cannot be undone.

Questions: support@divzplit.com. Read the Privacy Policy.
