DivZplit helps people organize shared expenses, balances and activities. It records shared information; it does not send money or carry out bank transfers.

Invitation links can be used by anyone who receives them: share them privately, and ask the administrator to replace or revoke a leaked link. DivZplit does not automatically contact your address book or send invitations on your behalf. Only upload content you have the right to share; avoid unnecessary personal details on receipts. 3. Expenses, payments and exchange rates

Amounts and expense splits depend on data entered by users. Check the results before sending money through another service. Marking a payment as sent or confirming it records a member's statement; DivZplit does not verify bank transfers. Payment simplification preserves net balances and suggests a distribution of transfers, but does not move funds. Currency conversions are indicative, depend on the source's availability and date, and may differ from your bank's rates or fees. Expenses and balances remain in the group's original currency.
