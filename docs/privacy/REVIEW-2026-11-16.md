### Terms of Use

Last updated: November 16, 2026

DivZplit helps people organize shared expenses, balances and activities. It records shared information; it does not send money or carry out bank transfers.

#### 1. Account and access

Use accurate account information and protect your credentials and devices. Do not access accounts or groups without authorization. Google sign-in is also subject to that provider's terms. Contact support if you notice unauthorized access.

#### 2. Groups, invitations and acceptable use

Group content is visible to its members. Administrators and authors can make changes within their permissions. Invitation links can be used by anyone who receives them: share them privately, and ask the administrator to replace or revoke a leaked link. DivZplit does not automatically contact your address book or send invitations on your behalf. Only upload content you have the right to share; avoid unnecessary personal details on receipts. Do not use the service for fraud, harassment, impersonation, unlawful content, infringement of rights or attempts to bypass security. Report harmful content or unauthorized access to support@divzplit.com with the relevant group or item and a brief explanation; never send passwords.

#### 3. Expenses, payments and exchange rates

Amounts and expense splits depend on data entered by users. Check the results before sending money through another service. Marking a payment as sent or confirming it records a member's statement; DivZplit does not verify bank transfers. Payment simplification preserves net balances and suggests a distribution of transfers, but does not move funds. Currency conversions are indicative, depend on the source's availability and date, and may differ from your bank's rates or fees. Expenses and balances remain in the group's original currency.

#### 4. Receipts, tasks and notifications

Automatic receipt recognition may contain errors: review and correct the data before saving. Deadlines and reminders help with organization but do not guarantee that a task will be completed or a notification will arrive on time. Delivery depends on permissions, connectivity and external services.

#### 5. Availability and changes

Features may change and the service may be interrupted by maintenance, failures or provider outages. Export information you need to keep. Material changes to these terms will be communicated through the service or account email as appropriate, with the revised version and date available here. Where required, we will request renewed acceptance. These terms do not remove any rights you have in relation to changes to a digital service.

#### 6. Complaints, restrictions and liability

Check disputed expenses and payments with the members involved. For a service complaint, contact support@divzplit.com with enough information to investigate. We may restrict access where reasonably necessary to address unlawful activity, serious misuse or security risks; where appropriate, we will explain the reason and how to ask for review, unless doing so would undermine security or a legal obligation. These terms do not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limit mandatory consumer rights.

#### 7. Privacy and account closure

Data use is explained in the [Privacy Policy](https://divzplit.com/privacy/). You can request deletion from Account → Delete account or [the account deletion page](https://divzplit.com/delete-account/). If the action fails or is unavailable, contact support. Once deletion succeeds, the old account loses access and its profile is cleared; shared balances and records remain under the label “Cuenta eliminada”. Account deletion does not cancel debts or erase every mention of you in shared descriptions, receipts or past records. Ask support to review remaining personal information and explain any justified retention. Clearing notifications, uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage does not delete your server account.

#### 8. Applicable law and contact

DivZplit is operated from Romania and these terms are governed by Romanian law. If mandatory consumer protections in your country of habitual residence apply, you retain those protections. Nothing here requires you to give up a competent court or complaint route available by law. Questions and complaints: [support@divzplit.com](mailto:support@divzplit.com).

#### 9. Age, advertising and future purchases

You must be at least 16 to register. No under-16 registration route through a guardian is offered. Applicable rules on contractual capacity still apply to users aged 16–17. A store content rating is separate from the minimum registration age. DivZplit currently has no advertising or paid features. Ads, a one-time purchase to remove ads, and premium features are possible future additions; none is purchased by accepting these terms. Before introducing them we will explain the features, price, payment type, store terms and applicable cancellation or refund rights, and update the privacy information and any required consent controls. A one-time purchase is not a recurring subscription. Account acceptance and push permission are not advertising consent.

#### 10. Service provider

DivZplit is provided by Adrian Roman, an individual based in Romania. Contact: [support@divzplit.com](mailto:support@divzplit.com). These terms cover [divzplit.com](https://divzplit.com/) and the DivZplit app, including distribution through an app store when available. The store’s own terms apply separately to its services.

### Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains which data DivZplit uses to provide accounts, shared groups and expense management, and how to contact us to exercise your rights.

#### 1. Data we use

- **Account:** email address, display name, user identifier, language, self-declared age band, terms version and acknowledgement details, and technical authentication data. If you choose Google sign-in, DivZplit receives the basic identity data you authorize, such as your identifier, name and email address. DivZplit does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive or contacts.
- **Shared content:** group names and members, invitations, expenses, amounts, currencies, participants, budgets, repayments, payment requests, tasks, polls, shopping lists and activity history.
- **Receipts:** images you choose to attach and information you add to expenses. Automatic receipt recognition runs in your browser; an attached image may be saved in the group's storage.
- **Notifications:** preferences, language, push subscription and technical data needed to deliver notifications when you enable them.
- **Technical use and support:** browser storage for your session and app functionality. Providers may process IP addresses and technical security logs. If you contact support, your email address and message content will be processed.

#### 2. Why we use data and our legal bases

We process the account and group data needed to provide the features you request to perform our contract with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Without a valid account email and required account information, we cannot create or secure your account. Receipt uploads and optional content are your choice. For security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, proportionate retention of shared records and handling complaints, we rely where appropriate on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), balanced against your rights and the interests of other group members. We rely on legal obligations where a specific law requires retention or disclosure (Article 6(1)(c)). Optional push delivery requires your permission; where processing relies on consent (Article 6(1)(a)), you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Turning off push does not close your account. Accepting the terms acknowledges this notice; it is not blanket consent to data use or advertising.

We send account verification and password-reset emails to provide and secure the account, not as advertising. These necessary service messages do not require a marketing opt-in and do not include a marketing unsubscribe link. We do not use them to send promotions or re-engagement campaigns. Any future marketing emails will be assessed separately and will provide the permissions and opt-out required by applicable law; account creation and terms acceptance are not marketing permission.

#### 3. Sharing, invitations and your choices

Members can see the information shared in their group, including attached receipts. Group administrators and authors have different editing permissions. Invitations are links you generate and choose to share, including through WhatsApp or your device’s sharing tools; DivZplit does not read your address book or automatically message invitees. A link may be forwarded, so share it privately and revoke it if needed. The sharing service processes data under its own terms. Avoid uploading unnecessary identifiers, payment-card details, health details or other sensitive information. The operator may access information as needed to maintain the service, investigate incidents and handle rights requests. We disclose information to authorities where legally required. We do not use expense balances to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

#### 4. Service providers

- **Supabase:** authentication, database, receipt storage and server functions. The primary database is hosted in Ireland (eu-west-1); this does not mean every provider operation remains in the EU.
- **GitHub Pages:** website hosting.
- **Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.):** our email-delivery processor for account verification and password-reset messages sent through Supabase. This involves the recipient email address, the message content (including its verification link or recovery code), delivery metadata and a name if included in the message. Resend states that customer data is stored and primarily processed in the United States; choosing an EU sending region does not make storage EU-only. Its [Data Processing Addendum](https://resend.com/legal/dpa) incorporates EU Standard Contractual Clauses for applicable international transfers. See its [data-location and transfer information](https://resend.com/security/gdpr); contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.
- **Google:** optional sign-in. Support email uses Google's email service.
- **Cloudflare:** domain management and support email forwarding.
- **Browser or device push services:** notification delivery if you enable it.

Exchange-rate requests use reference sources from the ECB and ExchangeRate-API without needing the group's personal expenses or balances. Providers may operate in different countries. Processing and international transfers must have the safeguards required by applicable law and are subject to each provider's contracts and terms.

#### 5. How long we keep data and what deletion does

We keep active account data while your account is used to provide the service. Shared expense records, balances, history and receipt references may remain while the group uses them or where necessary for a specific legal obligation or dispute. We use the continuing need for these purposes to assess retention. You can request a review of records that are no longer needed.

Choose Account → Delete account or [the deletion page](https://divzplit.com/delete-account/), sign in and confirm the account email. If the action fails or is unavailable, contact support. Once it succeeds, the account is disabled, its sign-in profile is cleared and its notification and push records are removed. Shared groups retain a technical account identifier and records needed for balances, with the member label “Cuenta eliminada”. This is de-identification of the displayed profile, not a guarantee that all remaining content is anonymous. Descriptions, receipt images and past records may still contain personal information. Groups with no other member are removed from the app; removal of associated stored files also needs completion through the storage provider. Contact support for a review of remaining information, receipt files or a wider erasure request.

Clearing notifications, uninstalling the app or clearing browser data does not erase your server account. Copies previously downloaded by other members are outside our direct control. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request and address an identified obligation or dispute. Provider logs and backups follow the relevant service’s retention and deletion cycles; deletion from active systems is not immediate deletion from every backup. Ask support for information about the retention that applies to your request.

Scheduled daily cleanup removes notifications older than 90 days, completed or discarded push-delivery jobs older than 7 days, disabled push subscriptions unchanged for 30 days, exchange-rate quotes older than 2 days, and invitations expired for over 30 days. The daily schedule can add up to one run interval. These rules do not automatically delete shared expenses, confirmed payments, group history or receipt images.

#### 6. Your rights and how to ask

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw consent where used. Object to processing based on legitimate interests by explaining your circumstances; we will assess the applicable grounds. Email [support@divzplit.com](mailto:support@divzplit.com). We ask only for proportionate identity verification where needed; do not send passwords or identity documents unless specifically requested through a suitable secure channel. Account → Download my account data provides an export when the account export feature is available. If it is unavailable, request a copy from support. Receipt image files, support correspondence and provider data are not necessarily included in the download. We consider other people’s rights before disclosing shared records. We respond without undue delay and normally within one month of receiving a request. If a lawful extension is needed for complexity or number of requests, we will explain it within the first month. You may complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work or where the alleged infringement occurred, including Romania’s [ANSPDCP](https://www.dataprotection.ro/).

#### 7. Browser storage and security

Local storage, session storage and service-worker storage support sign-in, language preferences, app or demo data and push account binding. Their duration depends on their purpose, sign-out or replacement and browser settings; some local data remains until removed. The reviewed app code has no third-party advertising or analytics integration. This does not mean hosting, email, sign-in and push providers collect no technical logs. Optional tracking will require separate disclosure and any permission required by law before activation. Clearing browser data can remove local information and sign you out; it does not delete the server account. We use HTTPS and account and group access controls. No service can guarantee absolute security; report suspected unauthorized access to support.

#### 8. Updates and contact

This policy will be updated when features or data processing change. The update date appears at the top. Questions and requests can be sent to [support@divzplit.com](mailto:support@divzplit.com).

#### 9. Age and future advertising or purchases

Registration is for people aged 16 or older. We collect a self-declared age band, not a date of birth; this is not independently verified age assurance. There is no under-16 registration route through a guardian. Report suspected underage accounts to support. Store content ratings do not change the registration age, and users aged 16–17 remain entitled to applicable protections for minors. Ads and paid features are not currently active. Terms acceptance and notification permission do not authorize advertising tracking. Store purchases, if introduced, will be subject to the disclosed payment arrangement and store rules.

Website: [divzplit.com](https://divzplit.com/). This notice covers the website and the DivZplit app, including app-store distribution when available.

### Help Center — 22 answers

#### How do I invite someone to my group?

The group administrator can generate an invitation and share the link. The other person must sign in and accept it. Share the link only with people you want to add.

#### How do I recover access to my account?

On the sign-in form, choose Forgot your password? and use your account email. If you sign in with Google, use the same Google account. Check your spam folder if a recovery message does not arrive.

#### Why do I need to confirm my email?

If you register by email, open the confirmation link before signing in. Check spam and the address you entered. A registration message alone does not confirm that an account exists. Use the latest link and keep sign-in codes private. Verification and password recovery are necessary account messages, not promotions; no marketing subscription or marketing unsubscribe link is needed for them. If you did not request a code, do not share it. Request a new one if it expires.

#### What does DivZplit access when I sign in with Google?

Sign-in requests basic identity information such as your name and email. It does not request Gmail, Drive or contacts access, and DivZplit does not receive your Google password. Use the same Google account to sign in again. If access fails, contact support without sending passwords.

#### What if an invitation link reaches the wrong person?

Anyone who receives the link can try to join using their account. Ask the administrator to disable current invitations or generate a new one; a new invitation invalidates previous links. Revoking a link does not remove people who already joined. Review membership and report unauthorized access to support. Invitations are not automatically sent to your address book.

#### How do I add an expense and choose who paid?

Press + and choose Add expense. Enter the amount, description, payer and people sharing it. Your account is selected as the initial payer, but you can choose someone else.

#### Is a budget required?

No. The group budget is optional. You can leave it empty and still record expenses and settle balances.

#### Can I add a receipt photo?

Yes. You can attach a receipt photo in the expense options. Automatic recognition can make mistakes: review the amount and other details before saving. The attached image is shared with group members.

#### What do You are owed and You owe mean?

You are owed means your net balance is in your favour. You owe means you need to repay money within the group. The balance depends on expenses, splits and confirmed payments.

#### Does DivZplit send money? Why is a payment pending?

DivZplit does not send money or make transfers. Pay through your bank or another service, then mark the payment as sent. The recipient must confirm receipt before balances update. They can report that the payment was not received.

#### What does Simplify payments do?

It suggests fewer transfers while preserving what each person owes or should receive. It does not change expenses or move money. Pending requests are already taken into account so the same payment is not requested twice.

#### How do I convert a balance to another currency?

Open Settle balances and choose a target currency. The original group currency remains on the left. Conversion uses the latest available reference rate and does not change the expenses’ currency. Your bank may apply a different rate or fees.

#### How do I enable notifications on my iPhone?

Add DivZplit to your Home Screen from Safari, then open it from that icon. Open the bell, go to Preferences and enable notifications. If you previously blocked permission, check your device settings.

#### Can I remind my group about the shopping list?

Yes. Open the shopping list and use Remind group when needed. Reminders are optional and respect each person’s notification preferences. There is a cooldown between reminders to avoid repeated messages.

#### Can I set deadlines and reminders?

Yes. When creating or editing a task or poll, you can set the available dates and reminders. Check your device time zone. Notification delivery depends on permissions, connectivity and each person’s preferences.

#### How do I download my data or delete my account?

In Account, choose Download my account data. If the download is unavailable, ask support for a copy; receipt files and other external data may need separate review. To close the account, choose Delete account and confirm your email, or open divzplit.com/delete-account/. If it fails, contact support. Once completed, the old account loses access and its profile is cleared. Shared groups keep balances and history under “Cuenta eliminada”; descriptions and receipts can still contain personal information. Groups where you are the only member are removed from the app. Ask support to review remaining data or files. Deleting an account does not cancel debts; uninstalling the app does not delete the account.

#### What is the minimum registration age?

Registration is available to people aged 16 or older. There is no registration route for under-16s through guardian authorization.

#### How long are expenses and receipts kept?

Active data is kept to provide the service, and shared records may still be needed by the group. Account deletion does not erase every mention in descriptions, history or receipts, or copies downloaded by other members. Ask support to review data that is no longer needed and explain any retention. Immediate erasure from provider backups is not guaranteed.

#### How do I request correction or erasure of personal data?

Email support@divzplit.com from your account address and identify the information to review. Proportionate verification may be needed. Do not send passwords or identity documents without a specific request through a secure channel. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, we explain it within that time. Contact opens your email app: you must send the message yourself.

#### Does uninstalling or clearing browser data delete my account?

No. Uninstalling or clearing browser data may sign you out or remove local data, but does not delete the server account. Demo data may exist only in that browser. To close an account, use Delete account and check the confirmation, or contact support if it fails.

#### How do I report a bug, abuse or unauthorized access?

Contact support@divzplit.com. Include the group or item, a brief explanation and approximate time; hide unrelated personal details in screenshots. If you suspect account access, secure the connected email or Google account too. Do not send passwords or codes. Support cannot verify bank transfers: check payments with your bank and the recipient.

#### Are there ads, purchases or subscriptions?

DivZplit currently has no ads or paid features. Ads, a one-time purchase to remove them and possible premium features are planned for consideration. They will be explained before introduction, including price, store terms and applicable rights. A one-time purchase is not a recurring subscription. Accepting the terms or enabling notifications does not buy anything or authorize advertising tracking.
