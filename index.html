WHATEVER COMES NEXT

Life is shared in so many ways.

Make DivZplit part of yours.

01Shared expenses

Every expense, clearly split.

Record what you shared, who paid and who took part. Check the group’s expenses and the balances calculated from them.

02Shopping lists

What you need, where everyone can see it.

Add products and quantities, mark purchases and prepare an expense from your shopping. Review the amount and split before saving.

03Polls

A plan you choose together.

Suggest options, vote and check the group’s answers. Each participant can change their vote while the poll remains open.

04Currency conversion

Another currency. The same clear picture.

Choose the group’s currency and view a conversion when settling up. Rates are a guide: your bank may use another rate or charge fees. Original amounts stay unchanged.

05Groups and trips

A space for every plan.

Organize trips, home or plans with friends in separate groups. Participants, dates, expenses and budgets stay with their own group.

06Tasks

Share what needs doing, too.

Assign tasks to participants, add deadlines and mark completed work. Make organizing the plan a shared effort.

07Budgets

A little foresight. More room to enjoy.

Set an optional budget and check spending, what remains and the breakdown by category. You can also track expenses without setting a limit.

IN CASE YOU WERE WONDERING

Before your next plan.

Is DivZplit free?+

Yes. You can create an account and use DivZplit’s current features for free.

Does the app send money?+

No. DivZplit records expenses and repayments but does not make transfers. Pay through your bank or another service, and the recipient confirms the payment.

Can I use different currencies?+

Yes. Choose from the supported currencies when creating a group and view a conversion when settling balances. Original amounts stay unchanged; the reference rate may differ from your bank’s rate.

Do I need to download an app?+

You can use DivZplit in your browser. If you prefer, add it to your phone’s Home Screen to open it as a web app.

Which languages are available?+

DivZplit is available in Spanish, English and Romanian. Choose a language on this page and the app will take that preference into account.
